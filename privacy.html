Liebehome privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses it

Liebehome is a private assistant for one household. Only the household's own Google account signs in to it. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the account holder's permission, Liebehome accesses that Google account's:

  • Gmail: it reads email and saves drafts. It never sends email; a person reviews and sends every draft.
  • Google Calendar: it lists the calendars, and reads, adds and changes events.
  • Google Drive: it reads documents. It does not change or delete them.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the household's own requests, such as summarising new email, keeping track of appointments or finding a document. To do that, it is processed by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude). The assistant runs on the household's own computer, and what it keeps stays there.

The data is not sold or shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.

Liebehome's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The account holder can remove Liebehome's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
